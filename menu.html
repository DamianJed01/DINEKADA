About Us

The story behind DINEKADA.

More Than a Meal. A Legacy Through Generations.

The name DINEKADA brings together an invitation and a heritage.

DINE

from Dito, "here"

An invitation to dine with us and experience the cuisine of Batangas.

KADA

from Dekada, "decade"

A reminder of the decades of culinary heritage and timeless food traditions of Batangas.
