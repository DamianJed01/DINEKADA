Our Origin Story

DINEKADA began with a simple wish: that the food of Batangas be served with the same care it is cooked with at home.

The name comes from two words. Dine, from dito, is our invitation to sit and eat here. Kada, from dekada, stands for the years behind every recipe on our menu. Together they say what we hope to offer: a place to sit down, and a taste of everything that came before.

From the start, we wanted a dish like bulalo or kalderetang kambing to be treated with respect, not reshaped to follow a trend. We would rather serve a recipe the way Batangas knows it, in a room where people can slow down and enjoy it.

Many of these dishes live in family kitchens and in memory. By keeping them on our menu, we hope to keep them in conversation, so younger guests learn their names and older guests find them just as they remember.

Local Suppliers and Ingredient Sourcing

Wherever we can, we buy from Batangas growers and fishers, because these dishes taste best close to where they began.

Bukid ni Mang Ruding, Lipa City: Kapeng Barako beans for our coffee.

Talisay Lakeshore Fishers' Group, Talisay: tawilis from Taal Lake, when in season.

Aling Rosing's Cacao Farm, Mabini: cacao for our Batangas chocolate.

The Dining Room

Our dining room captures the warmth and charm of Batangas, combining traditional Filipino elements with a touch of timeless elegance. Soft, warm lighting creates a cozy and welcoming atmosphere, while natural wooden finishes, woven accents, capiz-inspired details, and photographs showcasing the beauty and heritage of Batangas bring the local culture into the space. Every table is thoughtfully arranged to provide comfort and room for meaningful conversations. Inspired by the rich traditions, flavors, and hospitality of Batangas, our restaurant is a place for long lunches, family celebrations, and quiet dinners for two. Here, every visit feels like coming home to Batangas.
